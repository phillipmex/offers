TariffMemo

A documented HTS classification memo per SKU — the "reasonable care" paper trail behind the duty rate you pay.

The 6-digit HS subheading on a shipping record isn't the 10-digit line your entries actually clear at — and in the current schedule that line decides both the base Column 1 duty rate and whether a Section 301 or IEEPA reciprocal add-on applies on top. Importers are responsible for classifying with reasonable care (19 USC 1592), and the practical evidence of that is a written record of how the classification was reached. Most small importers don't have one.

TariffMemo is that record, per SKU:

  • Candidate 10-digit line — the specific HTS line the SKU most plausibly clears at, worked from the schedule's own legal notes and headings, cited to hts.usitc.gov.
  • Rate build-up — the Column 1 general rate for that line plus any Chapter 99 add-on (Section 301 lists, IEEPA reciprocal) that attaches to it, so you see the whole bill, not just the base rate.
  • The reasoning in writing — a memo you can hand to your customs broker or counsel as a documented starting point, instead of a verbal "we've always used that code."
