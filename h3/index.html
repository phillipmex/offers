A drafted, ready-to-distribute Consumer Confidence Report for your water system — flat price, no calls, no subscription.

Every community water system has to put a Consumer Confidence Report in front of its customers each year by July 1. It is not a hard document to produce, but it is a long one, and on a small system it usually lands on someone who is already running the plant, doing the billing and answering the phone. That is why the EPA SDWIS record shows so many small systems with a CCR reporting violation still open — not bad water, just a document nobody had time to assemble.

The rule is also changing. EPA's revised CCR requirements take effect in 2027, and they add plain-language requirements, a second delivery each year for larger systems, and specific handling for lead results. Systems that were already behind on the old form will be behind on the new one.

TapSheet is the drafting, done for you:

  • A complete draft CCR — built from your system's own published EPA data and the monitoring results you send me, laid out in the required order with the required mandatory language, ready to print or post.
  • Written to the 2027 rule — plain-language wording and the newer required elements, so the same document still works next cycle.
  • Delivered as a finished file — a PDF you review, sign and distribute, plus an editable version so your own numbers can be updated next year.
